The client should replace them with **commissioned photography of real Egyptian poultry operations**, together with the photo-rights paperwork and model releases. Until then, no image may imply that the farm, people, equipment or products shown belong to Alshorouk or its customers.

## Licence verification method

- **Unsplash:** we called `GET /napi/photos/<id>` from a real browser session. Every pick returned `premium: false` and `plus: false`. The public photo page states *"Free to use under the Unsplash License"*, and none of the picks showed the Unsplash+ licence. The originals were downloaded through the official `/photos/<id>/download?force=true` endpoint, which also registers the download with Unsplash.
- **Pexels:** the photo page's embedded data has `"license":"Pexels"` and links to `https://www.pexels.com/license/`. The originals were downloaded from `images.pexels.com` at full resolution.
- **Licence terms to respect:** no attribution is legally required on either platform, but we credit every photo in the registry and recommend a credits line in the footer or legal page. Neither licence allows selling unaltered copies, implying that the people or brands shown endorse Alshorouk, or using a photo as Alshorouk's own product or farm. **Neither licence includes model or property releases.**

### Slots with a partial fit (honest gaps)

- **`categoryVaccines`:** there is no licence-clean, documentary photo of *correct poultry vaccination*. Every "chicken / poultry vaccination" result on Unsplash and Pexels was human vaccination, usually with legible COVID-19 vial labels. A generic gloved-syringe still life is used in the meantime.
- **`farmExterior`:** no licensed dawn or sunrise exterior that clearly shows poultry houses passed the checks. A daylight aerial of US houses is used.
- **`statement`:** close-ups taken inside commercial houses showed comb scabs or peck marks, which a veterinary supplier should not show. A healthy village rooster portrait (Nepal) is used. Its setting is neutral, not commercial.

## Method

1. **Search.** We used 119 queries: the brief's list plus variants such as hatchery, setter, nipple drinker, poultry houses aerial, amber bottles and glass vials.
   - **Unsplash:** the `napi` search endpoint, run from a real Chromium session (Playwright 1.64.0 in the scratch folder, because unsplash.com sits behind an anti-bot challenge). 3,822 unique results; 735 were Unsplash+ or premium and were excluded automatically.
   - **Pexels:** the search pages' embedded data. 2,374 unique results.
   - **Pixabay:** probed but **not used**. Search works, but original downloads require a logged-in account (anonymous requests return HTML), and sponsored iStock results are mixed into the listings.
2. **Triage.** About 4,000 thumbnails were laid out in labelled grids by slot. The poultry-house, people and vet, chicks, eggs, feed, vials and exterior grids were reviewed by eye. About 70 shortlisted frames were then inspected at 1,100–3,600 px, with 100% zoom checks for species (turkey vs chicken), legible text, logos and labels, and bird condition (comb lesions, feather loss, distress).
3. **Hard-rejection rules applied:** no non-chicken animals (including turkeys, ducks and quail); no pets or backyard-hobby framing; no dead, sick, injured or processed birds; no distressing cages or overcrowding; no legible labels, logos or watermarks; no staged smiling poses; long edge at least 2,000 px; no heavy filters or AI-looking renders; correct context for each segment.

## Processing (web masters)

- Python 3.13 and Pillow. EXIF orientation is applied, then the image is converted to sRGB. All originals were either tagged sRGB or untagged and treated as sRGB, so no profile conversion was needed.
- Long edge is 2,400 px, or 2,800 px for `hero` and `heroAlt*`, resized with LANCZOS. The **full frame is kept** (no crop); framing is done through the `focal` values.
- JPEG, progressive, optimised, with **EXIF, ICC and GPS stripped** (the originals had no GPS). The default quality is 80. Where a very detailed frame exceeded its size target (600 KB, or 900 KB for heroes), quality was lowered in steps of 2: `heroAlt1` q70, `segmentBroilers` q68, `segmentLayers` q68, `farmExterior` q72, `categoryFeed` q74. A 100% check at q68 showed no visible blocking. All 19 files are within target.
- Focal points are CSS `object-position` percentages. Each was checked against 4:5 (arch window), 9:16 (mobile), 1:1 (sticky frame) and 16:9 cover crops.
